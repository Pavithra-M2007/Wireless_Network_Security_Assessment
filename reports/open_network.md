##### [CRITICAL] ENC-001 — Open Network — No Encryption

**Evidence:** `cipher = 'NONE'`

**Explanation:** No RSN Information Element and no Privacy capability bit were observed in any beacon from this AP. All frames are transmitted in plaintext and client associations are unauthenticated.

**Impact:** Any passive observer on the same RF channel can capture all traffic including credentials, session tokens, and sensitive data without specialised hardware or an active attack.

**Remediation:** Enable WPA3-Personal (SAE) or at minimum WPA2-Personal (AES/CCMP). For shared-access networks consider OWE (Opportunistic Wireless Encryption, AKM 18) which provides per-session encryption without a shared passphrase.
