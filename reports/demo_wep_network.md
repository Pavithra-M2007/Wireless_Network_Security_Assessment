##### [CRITICAL] ENC-002 — WEP Encryption Detected

**Evidence:** `cipher = 'WEP'`

**Explanation:** The AP sets the Privacy capability bit in its beacons but advertises no RSN Information Element. This combination indicates WEP (Wired Equivalent Privacy), deprecated since IEEE 802.11i-2004 and cryptographically broken.

**Impact:** WEP keys can be recovered in under 60 seconds with freely available tools given approximately 5 000 captured initialisation vectors. All WEP-protected traffic is effectively transmitted in plaintext.

**Remediation:** Replace WEP immediately with WPA3-Personal (preferred) or WPA2-Personal (AES/CCMP). There is no configuration or firmware change that can make WEP cryptographically secure; hardware that does not support WPA2 must be retired or replaced.
